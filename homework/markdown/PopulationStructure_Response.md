**Population Structure - Song Sparrows**

*A) Individual Ancestry Proportions*

I created ADMIXTURE plots for three values of K, two through six. I wanted to include six because the paper described that the sampling included
six morphologically distinct sub species separated into two groups saltmarsh endemics and upland residents. I found that how you sort your 
individuals in the admixture stacked plots has a significant effect on how you may interpret them. I sorted all individuals based on clustering of
K = 2 and K = 6. K of six sorting revealed the six distinct clusters described in the paper, with one group appearing as separate from the others
at all 6 values of K.

*B)Cross-validation / BIC Values*

The cross-validation values for a K of two were lower than K three through six. In the BIC plot there is a clear elbow at a K of two as well. It
illustrates the strength of that separation of the single group from the other individuals. It would be interesting to consider what makes those
individuals unique.
