**Population Structure - Song Sparrows**

*A) Individual Ancestry Proportions*

I created ADMIXTURE plots for three values of K, two through six. I wanted to include six because the paper described that the sampling included
six morphologically distinct sub species separated into two groups saltmarsh endemics and upland residents Considering the plots, my interpretation 
would be that the best supported K value would be two. Closer consideration at the individual level as to if those groups correspond to upland vs
salt marsh individuals would be my next step. 

My results contrast with Jason's. He mentioned that he utilized their VCF from the website. It would be interesting to investigate why my analysis
shows two populations with a gradient of genetic similarity, versus his work supporting the six separate population clusters.

*B)Cross-validation / BIC Values*

Additionally, the conclusion of K of two being the best supported value from my analysis is reflected in the cross-validation values and BIC values.
